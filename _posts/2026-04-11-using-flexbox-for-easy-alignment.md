I used Flexbox for row and column layouts instead of trying 
complex positioning. It made aligning cards and navigation 
links much simpler and cleaner.

One important thing I learned was about readability and 
spacing. Text is much easier to read with proper line-height 
and margin. Even small spacing changes can make a big visual 
difference on a webpage.

My beginner CSS checklist that I follow now:
- Use consistent spacing values
- Keep color palette small and clean
- Test layout on smaller screens
- Avoid too many effects and animations
