As the semester moved toward midterms, the pressure started building up 
in a way I had not expected. Dr. Bilal Ahmad introduced a system of regular 
quizzes to keep us on track with Programming Fundamentals. The first quiz 
was taken as a trial and was not graded, which helped us understand the 
format without the added stress of marks. After that, quizzes became a 
regular part of our Monday schedule, each carrying ten marks.

I appreciated the structure. Having quizzes at fixed intervals meant there 
was always a reason to stay consistent. Dr. Bilal also emphasized attendance 
strongly, and that discipline kept the class engaged and regular. But 
personally, I was still struggling. My programming concepts were weak, and 
no matter how much I tried to cram before each quiz, the gaps in my 
understanding kept showing up.
